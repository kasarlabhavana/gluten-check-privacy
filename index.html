Gluten Check – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 (updated for advertising)

In short: Gluten Check has no accounts and no analytics. Your scan history and settings stay on your phone. Photos of ingredient lists are read on your phone and deleted straight away; they are never uploaded. Barcode numbers are looked up in the free Open Food Facts database. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives device and advertising identifiers. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), you are asked for consent first.

Who we are

Gluten Check is a free Android app that reads ingredient information and labels to help people avoid gluten. It does not test food and cannot guarantee that a product is gluten-free. Contact: bharadwajraansh@gmail.com.

Information stored only on your phone

  • Scan history – your last 50 checked products, including product details from Open Food Facts and any ingredient text you typed or photographed.
  • Settings – your chosen region, whether you finished the welcome screens, and a hidden debug switch.
  • A scan counter – used only to ask once for a Google Play review after your third successful scan.

This information never leaves your phone. You can delete single history entries in History, or everything at once with Settings → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Camera

The app uses your camera to read barcodes and to photograph ingredient lists. Barcodes are read on your phone. Ingredient photos are turned into text on your phone using Google ML Kit's built-in text recognition, which works offline. Each photo is deleted immediately after the text is read. Photos are never uploaded, shared or kept.

Information sent over the internet

When you scan a barcode, the app sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open food database, to get the product's ingredients and labels. As Open Food Facts asks of all apps, each request names the app and includes the developer's contact email (not yours). Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your device's internet (IP) address when the request is made. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy.

Products you have checked before open from your phone's history without any network request.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google receives information from your device, such as your advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, and ad interactions. Your scan history, ingredient text and photos are never shared with Google or any advertiser.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in other places where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Google settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your region

The app reads the region from your phone's language and region settings (not your location or GPS) to explain what "gluten-free" means on labels where you shop. You can change it in Settings.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other apps or websites: an Open Food Facts product page, a food regulator's reporting page, your phone or email app, or the Google Play review window. Those services have their own privacy policies. The app does not send them any of your history.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or payments.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • No selling of personal information. Only the advertising information described above goes to Google.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone, location or photo gallery.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Results come from volunteer-maintained data and the text you provide, and may be wrong or out of date. Always check the package. The app is not medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date changed before the new version of the app is released.
